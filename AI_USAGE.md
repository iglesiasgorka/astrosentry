# AstroSentry — AI assistance disclosure

AstroSentry was developed with assistance from **OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex**.
ChatGPT supported planning, technical explanations, troubleshooting and draft
documentation. Codex generated and revised portions of Python and
React/TypeScript code, scientific-processing code, tests, browser automation,
deployment instructions, interface copy and documentation. This includes code
that computes derived scientific visualizations; assistance is not restricted
to cosmetic changes. Exact model versions are not asserted where unrecorded.

The project owner defined scope, supplied infrastructure and reported physical
tests, reviewed the interface and directs the final submission. The owner is
responsible for final review and scientific claims. Passing automated tests is
not independent validation of astrometric or photometric significance.

## Scientific data and media

Original observations are real SPHEREx archive products from IRSA. No generative
AI model fabricates FITS observations, source coordinates, measured positions or
missing data. Reprojection, masks, visual normalization and diagnostic outputs
are numerical operations in the disclosed AI-assisted implementation. Catalog
predictions are distinct from measurements; exploratory warnings remain intact.
The application runs no generative model or AI source-detection service.

This phase introduces text/code branding, not a generated astronomical image,
synthetic audio or synthetic video. Browser screenshots/recordings depict the
actual application and archive-derived imagery. No NASA insignia, official
mission logo or agency design asset has been added to AI-generated branding.
Scientific mission names and source credits identify the real datasets and do
not imply endorsement. If synthetic visuals/video or narration are added later,
disclose them separately before submission; the current record does not cover
unreviewed future media.

## Submission disclosure

The NASA Space Apps GO Team's [2026 AI guidance](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1849)
calls for disclosure in descriptions and metadata, labeling of generated media,
and keeping agency visual identity out of generated content. This project records
assistance here, in README, the About panel, HTML metadata and
frontend/public/project-metadata.json. The official resources page could not be
retrieved during this check; the dated GO Team answer was accessible.

Suggested submission statement:

> OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex assisted planning, implementation, testing and
> documentation. The displayed observations originate from real SPHEREx/IRSA
> data. Derived numerical results retain their provenance and exploratory
> limitations; no generative model creates observations or validates detections.

The owner should also place this disclosure in the submission's AI/resources
fields. No team-page submission or publication was performed by the agent.
